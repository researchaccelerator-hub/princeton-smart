# Participant Guide — Princeton SMART

This guide is for people who have been asked to install the Princeton SMART app as part of a research study. It explains what the app does, what it collects, and how to get started.

---

## What is this app?

Princeton SMART is a research app that runs quietly in the background on your Android phone. It is used by researchers at [your institution] to study how people use their smartphones. The app was developed at Princeton University.

You have been invited to participate in a study led by [researcher name / study contact]. By installing this app, you help researchers understand patterns in smartphone use.

---

## What does the app collect?

While the app is running, it collects:

- **Screenshots** — a full-screen image taken approximately every 5 seconds
- **Text from screenshots** — text extracted automatically from those images (called OCR)
- **App usage logs** — which apps are open and when
- **App history** — a record of recent app switching (the last few apps you had open)
- **Accessibility data** — information about the layout and content of the screen
- **Session times** — when your phone screen turns on and off

---

## What does the app NOT collect?

The app **cannot** access:

- Your location
- Your microphone
- Your camera
- Your speakers or audio

---

## Where does my data go?

All data is sent directly to a private, secured cloud storage account belonging to the research team at [your institution]. It is not shared with any third party and does not go to any shared or public server.

---

## How to install the app

You will receive an email from Firebase App Distribution with a link to install the app. Tap the link on your Android phone and follow the prompts to install it. You do not need to download it from the Google Play Store.

Once installed, open the app and register with:
- Your email address
- The panel code provided by the research team: **[panel code]**

---

## One-time setup: granting permissions

After registering, the app will walk you through granting three permissions. Each one is required for the app to work. The app cannot grant these automatically; you will be taken to your phone's settings to enable each one.

**1. Usage access**
Allows the app to see which apps are open. Without this, the app cannot record app usage data.
- When prompted, tap **Grant Access**, find the app in the list, and enable the toggle.

**2. Battery optimization exemption**
Keeps the app running reliably in the background. Without this, Android may suspend the app to save battery.
- When prompted, tap **Grant Access** and select **Allow** or **Don't optimize**.

**3. Accessibility service**
Allows the app to read the content on your screen. Without this, screenshots cannot be linked to app content.
- When prompted, tap **Grant Access**, find the app in the list, and enable the toggle. Confirm any system warning that appears.

Once all three permissions are granted, the app is ready. Tap **Start** to begin the study.

---

## Day-to-day experience

After setup, the app runs silently in the background. You do not need to open or interact with it. A small notification will appear in your status bar to indicate the app is active. You can continue using your phone normally.

---

## Battery and performance

The app is designed to have minimal impact on battery life. The battery optimization exemption you granted during setup is what allows it to run efficiently without being suspended by Android.

---

## How to pause or stop

**To pause temporarily:** Open the app and use the pause option on the main screen.

**To stop participating:** You can stop the app at any time by opening it and pressing the stop button, or by going to **Settings > Accessibility** on your phone, finding the app in the list, and disabling it.

You can also uninstall the app at any time from your phone's app settings. Uninstalling stops all data collection immediately.
